Our process

A clear, considered path from idea to next step.

Starting a landscaping project can feel like a big decision. A straightforward process helps clarify what you need, what is possible and how to move forward.

Begin with an inquiry →
01

Tell us about your space

Complete the inquiry form with your location, project type, goals and any reference images that help explain your ideas.

02

Review and discuss

We review the information you provide and follow up to clarify the scope, priorities and practical details.

03

Confirm next steps

Once the scope and service availability are understood, we can discuss the suitable next step and any further information needed.

Helpful details to prepare

Approximate dimensions, your project location, a short wish list and a few reference photos can make the first conversation more useful.

No finished plan yet? That’s okay.

You do not need to have every detail decided. Start with what you want to improve and what you want the space to feel like.
